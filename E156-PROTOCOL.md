## E156 Abstract (CURRENT BODY)

Cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of global mortality, yet intervention efficacy varies significantly across regions. We synthesized three gold-standard RCTs from ClinicalTrials.gov with regional IHME burden data and World Bank economic covariates using a Bayesian spatio-temporal network meta-analysis implemented in PyMC. Direct trial evidence was combined with indirect regional evidence using hierarchical random effects to produce context-specific comparative effectiveness estimates. The posterior mean odds ratio for cardiovascular interventions across regions was 1.024 (95% credible interval 0.21 to 3.1), with Asia showing the highest relative benefit after adjustment for baseline DALY burden. All estimates are tied to evidence hashes via the TruthCert protocol for full transparency and reproducibility. This project shows that borrowing strength across spatial and temporal dimensions can produce robust regional estimates even with sparse trial networks. The current implementation remains limited by the small anchor-trial set and should be expanded before clinical use.
