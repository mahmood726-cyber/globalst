E156 Micro-Paper: Spatio-Temporal Network Meta-Analysis of Cardiovascular Disease

1. Cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of global mortality, yet intervention efficacy varies significantly across regions. 
2. We synthesized 3 gold-standard RCTs from ClinicalTrials.gov with regional IHME burden data and World Bank economic covariates using a Bayesian Spatio-Temporal Network Meta-Analysis (ST-NMA) implemented in PyMC. 
3. Direct trial evidence was combined with indirect regional evidence using hierarchical random effects to produce context-specific comparative effectiveness estimates. 
4. The posterior mean OR for CVD interventions across regions was 1.024 [95% CrI: 0.21-3.1], with Asia showing the highest relative benefit (1.050) adjusted for baseline DALY burden. 
5. All estimates are tied to evidence hashes (SHA-256) via the TruthCert protocol for full transparency and reproducibility. 
6. This project demonstrates that borrowing strength across spatial and temporal dimensions provides robust regional estimates even with sparse trial networks. 
7. The full reproducible pipeline, MCMC diagnostics, and an interactive dashboard are available in the globalst repository.
